Policy Title:
Unexpected Charge Policy

Policy ID:
REF-002

Version:
1.0

Domain:
Refunds & Charges

Applicable Intent:
UNEXPECTED_CHARGE

Issue:
Customer reports an unexpected charge or debit.

Agent Procedure:
1. Verify the customer and retrieve the relevant transaction.
2. Check transaction type, amount, date, merchant or charge description.
3. Determine whether the charge is a known fee, merchant transaction, or potentially unauthorized transaction.
4. Follow the applicable dispute or investigation process.

Resolution Guidance:
Do not label a transaction fraudulent without appropriate evidence and process.

Risk Level:
High

Human Escalation:
Escalate suspected unauthorized transactions according to the fraud procedure.

Agent Response Guidance:
Acknowledge the unexpected charge and explain that the transaction is being investigated.
