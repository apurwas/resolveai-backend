Customer reports that an expected refund has not yet been received.

Agent Procedure:
1. Retrieve the original transaction and refund reference if available.
2. Verify refund status, amount, date, and originating merchant or service.
3. Determine whether the refund has been initiated, processed, or rejected.
4. Follow the applicable refund investigation process.

Resolution Guidance:
Do not state that a refund is completed unless the refund status is verified.

Risk Level:
Medium

Human Escalation:
Escalate when a confirmed refund cannot be traced to the customer's account.

Agent Response Guidance:
Acknowledge the missing refund and explain that the refund status is being verified.
