ATM_PARTIAL_CASH

Issue:
Customer reports that the ATM dispensed less cash than the amount debited or requested.

Agent Procedure:
1. Retrieve the ATM transaction and verify requested amount.
2. Capture the amount actually dispensed if available.
3. Compare the transaction record with the customer's reported cash received.
4. Follow the applicable partial-dispense dispute process.

Resolution Guidance:
Do not assume the missing amount without verifying transaction and ATM records.

Risk Level:
High

Human Escalation:
Escalate when the transaction and ATM records cannot establish the dispensed amount.

Agent Response Guidance:
Acknowledge the partial-dispense issue and explain that transaction and ATM records are being checked.
