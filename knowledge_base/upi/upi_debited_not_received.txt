Policy Title:
UPI Debited but Merchant Did Not Receive Funds Policy

Policy ID:
UPI-004

Version:
1.0

Domain:
UPI

Applicable Intent:
UPI_DEBITED_NOT_RECEIVED

Issue:
Customer's account was debited but the merchant reports that the payment was not received.

Agent Procedure:
1. Retrieve and verify the UPI transaction.
2. Check transaction status, amount, timestamp, reference number, and beneficiary information.
3. Determine whether the transaction is successful, pending, failed, or reversed.
4. Follow the applicable dispute or reversal process based on the verified status.

Resolution Guidance:
Do not ask the customer to make another payment until the original transaction status is established.

Risk Level:
High

Human Escalation:
Escalate when transaction data is inconsistent, funds cannot be traced, or fraud is suspected.

Agent Response Guidance:
Acknowledge the debit and explain that the transaction status and recipient-side outcome are being investigated.
