UPI_PAYMENT_FAILED

Issue:
Customer reports that a UPI payment failed.

Agent Procedure:
1. Verify the customer and retrieve the relevant UPI transaction.
2. Check transaction status, amount, timestamp, and reference number.
3. Determine whether the transaction is failed, pending, reversed, or successful.
4. If the transaction is confirmed failed, follow the applicable retry or reversal process.

Resolution Guidance:
Do not ask the customer to make repeated payments until the transaction status has been verified.

Risk Level:
Medium

Human Escalation:
Escalate when transaction status is inconsistent or fraud is suspected.

Agent Response Guidance:
Acknowledge the failed payment and explain that the transaction status is being verified.
