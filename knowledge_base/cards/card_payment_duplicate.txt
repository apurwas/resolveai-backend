Duplicate Card Payment Policy

Cards

Applicable Intent:
CARD_PAYMENT_DUPLICATE

Issue:
Customer reports being charged more than once for the same purchase.

Agent Procedure:
1. Retrieve the relevant card transactions.
2. Compare merchant, amount, timestamp, and authorization references.
3. Determine whether the transactions are genuine duplicates or separate authorizations.
4. Follow the applicable duplicate-transaction resolution process.

Resolution Guidance:
Do not classify transactions as duplicates solely because the amounts are identical.

Risk Level:
Medium

Human Escalation:
Escalate when transaction records cannot establish whether the charges are duplicates.

Agent Response Guidance:
Acknowledge the duplicate-charge concern and explain that the transactions are being compared.
