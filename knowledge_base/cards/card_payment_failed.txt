Policy Title:
Card Payment Failed Policy

Policy ID:
CARD-001

Version:
1.0

Domain:
Cards

Applicable Intent:
CARD_PAYMENT_FAILED

Issue:
Customer reports that a card payment failed.

Agent Procedure:
1. Verify the customer and retrieve the card transaction.
2. Check authorization status, merchant information, amount, and timestamp.
3. Determine the reason or status of the failed authorization.
4. Follow the applicable card troubleshooting process.

Resolution Guidance:
Do not advise repeated attempts when the transaction may involve a fraud or security restriction.

Risk Level:
Medium

Human Escalation:
Escalate when the failure is unexplained or security controls may be involved.

Agent Response Guidance:
Acknowledge the failed card payment and explain that the transaction status is being checked.
